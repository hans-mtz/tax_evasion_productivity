# Proof-read report: ch. 7, P2 and P2b (draft, 24 Sept 2026)

Scope: the two new paragraphs replacing P2 ("why does this matter?"). Changes already applied to the version shown in chat.

## P2

| # | Original | Revised | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "matters for policy makers" | "matters for policymakers" | Standard single-word form. |
| 2 | "evasion changes the efficiency cost of setting tax rates" | "evasion affects the marginal efficiency cost of raising them" | Matches Slemrod's wording (p. 907); "marginal" is the precise concept. Avoids repeating "tax rates". |
| 3 | "the recent empirical literature has learned far more" | "recent empirical work says more" | A literature does not learn; "far more" is not quantified in the source. |
| 4 | "firms evade through reported costs" | "firms adjust reported costs" | Carrillo et al. (2017) and Asatryan & Peichl document offsetting cost reports, not proven evasion; avoids overclaiming. |
| 5 | "were made to report more revenue" | "when tax authorities pushed … to report more revenue" | Active voice; names the actor. |
| 6 | "96 and 70–80 cents per dollar" | "… per dollar of additional revenue" | Clarifies the denominator. |
| 7 | "offers a setting to study it" | "lets me study this question" | Active, names what is studied. |
